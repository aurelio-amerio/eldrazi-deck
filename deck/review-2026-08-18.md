# Zhulodok Bracket-3 Deck Review — 2026-08-18

Review of `deck/zhulodok-bracket3.txt`. Prices are Cardmarket EUR via Scryfall, snapshot
2026-08-18. Every recommendation here is checked against bracket 3: no Game Changers added
(Mana Vault stays the only one, 1/3), and `spellbook_find_decklist_combos` on the post-swap
list returns **`included: []`** — no infinite combos present.

---

## Verdict

The card *choices* are good — the problems are structural. Three things hold the deck back:

1. **Two mana rocks physically cannot produce `{C}`** in a deck with 12 colorless pips plus the
   commander's.
2. **The curve is top-heavy without enough cheap ways to get to 7.** 27 of 62 nonlands sit at
   MV 7+. The highest-leverage upgrade available is *cards with a huge mana value but a small
   real cost* — they hand you double cascade on turn 4–5.
3. **Zero protection, zero recursion.** One targeted removal spell answers a 10-mana threat and
   the deck has no reply.

All twelve recommended swaps together cost **12.06€** and free **12.81€** from the buy column,
so the buylist total goes *down* slightly (199.21€ → ~198.46€).

---

## What's already right (don't touch these)

- **Echoes of Eternity (0.75€) is the best card in the deck.** Its first ability reads
  "a triggered ability of a colorless **spell** you control" — cascade is a triggered ability of
  the spell, so with Echoes out, Zhulodok's *cascade, cascade* becomes **four cascades**, and it
  copies the titan on top. 0.75€ for the deck's ceiling.
- **All Is Dust is one-sided here.** Oracle text is "each player sacrifices all permanents they
  control that are one or more colors" — your Eldrazi, Wastes, rocks and Ugins all survive. This
  is a strength the deck should lean on harder (see swap #5).
- **Not of This World costs `{0}`.** Zhulodok is a 7/4, so it meets the "power 7 or greater"
  clause. Free protection for the linchpin.
- **Darksteel Monolith is the engine.** "Once each turn, you may pay `{0}` rather than pay the
  mana cost for a colorless spell you cast from your hand" = a free titan *with* double cascade
  every turn. It's in the proxy pile (20.51€) — that's fine, but treat it as the single most
  important card to actually have sleeved.
- The land base is fine. All 36 mana lands produce `{C}` (Eye of Ugin taps for nothing, as
  designed). Keeping the three Urza lands as Wastes-floor filler is consistent with the
  standalone-cards principle and costs nothing.

## Bugs found

### 1. `deck/bracket4-upgrades.md` recommends an illegal card

**Kozilek's Unsealing is `{2}{U}` — color identity blue.** Devoid makes it colorless but does
not change color identity. It cannot be played in a Zhulodok deck. This is the same trap the
spec already recorded as errata for Ugin's Binding.

Closest colorless replacements for that slot: **Endless Atlas** (1.35€ — 18 Wastes means it's
always online) or **Mind's Eye** (2.99€).

### 2. Roaming Throne does less than the list implies

Roaming Throne (36.67€ proxy) doubles triggered abilities "of another **creature you control**".
Cascade and "when you cast this spell" triggers belong to the *spell on the stack*, not to a
creature — so Roaming Throne doubles **neither**. It doubles annihilator and ETB triggers only.

That's still worth a slot, but the card doing the cascade-doubling work is Echoes of Eternity at
0.75€. Worth knowing before anyone pays 36€ for the Throne. (Abstruse Archaic *can* copy a
cascade trigger — its ability targets "a colorless source", which the spell is.)

### 3. Spawnsire of Ulamog's marquee ability is dead

`{20}: Cast any number of Eldrazi spells from among cards you own outside the game` does nothing
in Commander — there is no sideboard. It's a 10-mana 7/11 with annihilator 1. Cut.

### The idea behind swaps 6, 7 and 8

Cascade density is the deck; mana is the constraint. The fix is not fewer big spells — it's
**big spells that are cheap to cast**, because Zhulodok reads mana *value*, not mana *paid*.
The colorless pool for this is small and the deck was missing most of it:

| Card | MV | Real cost | € |
|---|---|---|---|
| Darksteel Monolith | 8 | `{0}`, once per turn | *(already in deck)* |
| Bygone Colossus | 9 | warp `{3}` | 0.27 |
| Metalwork Colossus | 11 | `{X}` less per noncreature artifact MV — often 2–4 | 0.34 |
| Excalibur, Sword of Eden | 12 | `{X}` less per historic permanent MV — often near 0 | 11.75 |

Bygone Colossus is in the twelve above. Metalwork Colossus is the obvious next one.

## Further options (not in the twelve)

- **Metalwork Colossus** (0.34€) — MV 11 for roughly 2–4 real mana once four rocks are down.
- **Matter Reshaper** (0.75€) — a 3-drop that replaces itself; raises the floor of your cascades.
- **Command Beacon** (7.49€) — Zhulodok back to hand with no commander tax. Genuinely strong in
  a deck this dependent on one 7/4 body, but it pushes the buy total ~5.7€ over 200€.
- **Steel Hellkite** (0.33€) / **Duplicant** (0.21€) — cheap colorless removal bodies at MV 6, so
  they also improve what your cascades hit.
- **Spine of Ish Sah** (0.21€) / **Meteor Golem** (0.17€) — removal at MV 7, i.e. removal that
  double-cascades.
- **Akroma's Memorial** (20.74€) — haste and trample for the whole Eldrazi board. Big effect,
  proxy-tier price. Cheaper partial answer: **Kaldra Compleat** (4.63€), which is also MV 7.
- **Chimil, the Inner Sun** (6.23€) — uncounterable spells plus a free discover 5 each end step.
- **Endless Atlas** (1.35€) — 18 Wastes make it unconditional draw.
- **Skittering Invasion** (0.42€) — an MV 7 sorcery that ramps five and double-cascades.

**Deliberately not recommended:** Glaring Fleshraker (belongs to the bracket-4 module with Echoes
of Eternity — adding it here blurs the bracket line you drew), Temple of the False God, Perilous
Vault (symmetrical, and All Is Dust is already asymmetric in your favour).

Ulalek, Fused Atrocity — Deck Primer

Covers ulalek-bracket3.txt B3 and ulalek-bracket4.txt B4 · combos verified on Commander Spellbook and win turns measured by a 20,000-game Monte Carlo sim per list, 2026-08-24.

◇What this deck is

Ulalek costs {C/W}{C/U}{C/B}{C/R}{C/G} — five hybrid pips, each payable with any color or true colorless. Any five mana casts it, and its five-color identity legalizes devoid cards Zhulodok can never play. Its text is the whole deck:

Whenever you cast an Eldrazi spell, you may pay {C}{C}. If you do, copy all spells you control, then copy all other activated and triggered abilities you control.

Two consequences drive everything below:

  • Every Eldrazi spell is double-dipped. Pay {C}{C} and the spell is copied (a permanent-spell copy becomes a token) and every cast trigger on the stack is duplicated — Ulamog's exile, Kozilek's draw, Elder Deep-Fiend's tap-four becomes tap-eight.
  • Copies are not cast. Token copies don't retrigger "when you cast" abilities. That is why every infinite line needs a trigger copier (Echoes of Eternity, Strionic Resonator) plus a Spawn-token payoff that turns each loop into more {C} than the {C}{C} it costs.

Bracket 3 — Battlecruiser

Wins by combat with doubled haymakers. Its two combo lines are late-game garnish: combo decides under 2% of goldfish games.

98% combat · 2% combo (sim, by T20)

Bracket 4 — Combo web

Same chassis, but the win moves to a 13-line combo web plus tutors, fast mana, and free interaction. Combat is the fallback.

76% combat · 22% combo — a floor; see caveat below

  • B4 Identical opening; from T5 the tutors assemble a copier + payoff pair around Ulalek. A protected combo turn typically lands T6–9, with combat pressure covering disrupted games.

◇Notable combos

Shared by both versions

Ulalek + Echoes of Eternity + Kozilek's Unsealing → infinite Spawns, infinite {C}, infinite ETB/death triggers, and (via Unsealing's second mode) drawing the deck.
Cast any Eldrazi creature of MV 4–6 with {C}{C} spare. Echoes doubles Ulalek's and Unsealing's triggers. Resolve one Ulalek trigger paying {C}{C}: it copies the spell and every other trigger on the stack. The doubled Unsealing triggers make four Spawns; sacrifice two to repay {C}{C} on the copied Ulalek trigger; each cycle nets +2 Spawns and re-copies everything.
MV 4–6 starters in the 99: Thought-Knot Seer, Sowing Mycospawn, Conduit of Ruin, a recast Ulalek — plus Azlask in B3, Spawn-Gang Commander and Writhing Chrysalis in B4. Elder Deep-Fiend is MV8 even via emerge; Ultima and Skittering Cicada aren't Eldrazi.

Basalt Monolith + Forsaken Monument → infinite {C}. Monument makes Basalt tap for 4 and untap for 3. Not an instant win — outlets: cast your whole hand, Kozilek the Great Distortion (refill to 7, keep casting), huge Kozilek's Command / Blast Zone / Eye of Ugin activations, Eldrazi Displacer machine-gun blinks.

B4 The combo web

The web is a grid — Ulalek + one copier + one payoff = infinite. Nearly every pairing works (13 Spellbook-verified lines): redundancy instead of cEDH tutor density.

  • Cleanest kill — Ulalek + Resonator + Spawn-Gang Commander ({5}{C}{C}{R}{R} all-in from hand): cast Spawn-Gang, Resonator copies Ulalek's trigger, each loop makes a token Spawn-Gang + 3 Spawns; sacrifice Spawns to Spawn-Gang's ability for infinite damage.
  • Fleshraker turns any line lethal: every Spawn that enters pings all three opponents, so "infinite tokens" becomes infinite damage with Fleshraker merely on the battlefield.
  • Fringe redundancy: Ulalek + Writhing Chrysalis + Eldritch Immunity, and + Abstruse Archaic (whose {1},{T} copies Ulalek's trigger like a one-shot Resonator; Rings of Brighthearth doubles the activation).
  • Food Chain + Eternal Scourge → infinite creature-only mana: exile Scourge for 4, recast from exile for 3, repeat. + Glaring Fleshraker = immediate infinite damage — the deck's fastest line (~T4 with a tutor). Scourge exiling itself when targeted is upside here.
  • Infinite-mana backups: Basalt + Rings of Brighthearth; Grim Monolith + Rings + Forsaken Monument. Same outlets as Basalt + Monument.

Combo-turn protection: lead with Cavern of Souls naming Eldrazi (the trigger spell can't be countered); keep Fierce Guardianship / Deflecting Swat / Stubborn Denial up (ferocious is nearly always on); Skittering Cicada gives colorless spells flash — combo on an opponent's end step. Deflecting Swat also retargets removal aimed at Ulalek or Echoes.

◇Notable interactions

The {C}{C} copy menu

  • Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger: exile four permanents (both cast triggers resolve even though the token copy dies to the legend rule).
  • Ulamog, the Defiler: two "exile top half of library" triggers — two different opponents, or ¾ of one player's deck.
  • Kozilek (both): doubled refill/draw triggers.
  • Elder Deep-Fiend: flash, tap eight permanents on cast — fizzle an attack or tap down two players. Emerge off a Spawn is a discount, not a requirement.
  • World Breaker: exile two artifacts/enchantments/lands; recurs itself for {2}{C} + a land.
  • Emrakul, the World Anew: steal two players' boards for the turn (madness for six {C} is the normal casting mode).
  • Kozilek's Command: the copy keeps X — all four modes scale double.
  • Ugin's Binding: bounce two permanents; from the graveyard, every colorless 7+ spell you cast exiles it for a one-sided mass bounce.
  • Desecrate Reality: up to two even-MV exiles per opponent + double Adamant reanimation.
  • Zhulodok in the 99: your 7+ colorless spells from hand get cascade ×2 — and Ulalek copies the cascade triggers too. One Ulamog with both legends out is four cascades deep.

Engine & utility

  • Sanctum of Ugin: any colorless 7+ spell → fetch any colorless creature to hand. B3 chains haymakers; B4 fetches combo creatures (Fleshraker, Spawn-Gang, Eternal Scourge are all colorless).
  • Eye of Ugin: −2 on every colorless Eldrazi spell; the late-game {7},{T} activation is a repeatable colorless-creature tutor.
  • Conduit of Ruin + Mystic Forge B4: fetch the 7+ creature to the top, cast it off the top — often the same turn, with the discounts stacked.
  • Kozilek's Unsealing is the best engine even off-combo: MV 4–6 creatures make 2 Spawns, 7+ draw 3. With Echoes, doubled — 4 Spawns or draw 6.
  • Eldrazi Displacer + Thought-Knot Seer: repeatable hand-strip (TKS is a real ETB). Displacer also resets Ulamog the Defiler's counters, untaps the team out of Deep-Fiend mode, or blanks attackers. Most haymakers have cast triggers — Displacer does not rebuy those.
  • All Is Dust sacrifices colored permanents — your devoid board survives. Adjudicated caveat: it also pops your own Imprisoned in the Moon. Sweep first, moon second (same for Ugin −X).
  • Warping Wail counters sorceries — most board wipes at these tables.
  • B3 Path of Annihilation + Azlask: Path gives every Eldrazi "{T}: any color", which is what makes Azlask's {W}{U}{B}{R}{G} mass-pump / annihilator activation reachable in a colorless deck.
  • B3 From Beyond: upkeep Spawn ramp; sacrifices into a tutor for any Eldrazi card — including Echoes of Eternity (Kindred Eldrazi enchantment), i.e. half the combo. It cannot fetch Kozilek's Unsealing (devoid, not an Eldrazi card).
  • Ugin's Labyrinth wants a 7+ colorless card imprinted from hand (20+ hits in both lists) — don't fetch it when your hand is all small.
  • Eldritch Immunity overloaded ({4}{C}): the whole team gets protection from each color — ignore colored blockers, removal, and sweepers on the swing turn.

◇Top-priority cards

Protect these, in order:

  • 1 · Ulalek — every plan runs through the copy trigger. Lightning Greaves the turn it lands; it eats every removal spell at the table. (Not of This World can't protect a naked Ulalek — its free mode needs power 7.)
  • 2 · Echoes of Eternity — the strongest permanent in either list: doubles every trigger and copies every colorless spell, combo or not. In B4 it's the #1 tutor target; informed opponents kill it on sight.
  • 3 · Kozilek's Unsealing — the draw engine; quietly wins long games alone.
  • 4 · Eye of Ugin / Ancient Tomb / Eldrazi Temple — the difference between T4 and T6 Ulalek; enemy Wastelands come here.
  • 5 B4 Mystic Forge + The One Ring — the grind engines that refuel a disrupted combo turn.
  • 6 · Forsaken Monument — team +2/+2, {C} doubling, half an infinite-mana line.

What opponents will (correctly) target: Echoes, Ulalek, Forsaken Monument; in B4 also Food Chain and Resonator. Deploy the second combo piece the turn you go off, not turns earlier.

Opening hand: 2–4 lands including a Tomb/Temple-class land or a 2+ mana rock, plus a cost reducer or an engine piece (Unsealing / Shadow in the Warp / B4 tutor). A hand that casts Ulalek by T4 with one engine behind it is a keep; five haymakers is not. Mulligan for the curve, not the top end.

Crop Rotation at instant speed: end-of-turn Sanctum with a haymaker in hand, or an on-the-spot Cavern against counter mana. Kicked Sowing Mycospawn also Strip-Mines a problem land.

B4 Black tutors — Demonic / Vampiric / Wishclaw

  • Ulalek out + a payoff in play → Echoes of Eternity (wins with any Eldrazi spell in hand).
  • Ulalek + Echoes out → cheapest payoff: Kozilek's Unsealing (an engine even if disrupted) or Spawn-Gang Commander (spell + payoff in one card).
  • No board, T2–3 → fast mana (Sol Ring / Mana Vault) or Food Chain (Scourge follows; Sanctum/Eye convert into Fleshraker for the kill).
  • Repeatedly disrupted → The One Ring, grind, reassemble — the web is redundant enough.

Vampiric Tutor: end of the turn before your combo turn. Wishclaw: activate only on the turn you win, so the opponents' borrow never happens.

Creature tutors

Conduit of Ruin only fetches MV 7+ colorless creatures — default Ulamog the Ceaseless Hunger (removal + clock) or Kozilek the Great Distortion (refill); with Mystic Forge out the topped card is castable immediately. Sanctum / Eye of Ugin fetch any colorless creature: B3 chains haymakers; B4 fetches Fleshraker → Spawn-Gang → Eternal Scourge when a combo is in reach.

◇Piloting by phase

T1–3

Land, rock, reducer. Only green pips (Nature's Lore / Beast Within, ~19 sources) and B4 black (tutors) matter — the rainbow lands and Talisman of Dominance exist for exactly that. Mox Diamond only with a spare land in hand.

T4–5

Ulalek + Greaves. Hold Warping Wail / Stubborn Denial when possible — the adjudicated danger window is T5–8 as archenemy, not the early game. In B4, tutor per the priorities above but bank the second combo piece in hand.

T6+

B3 Every Eldrazi cast comes with {C}{C} held. Sequence: threat with cast trigger → copy → Sanctum fetch → next threat. Eldritch Immunity and Cavern casts break through interaction; All Is Dust resets go-wide boards and mostly misses you. Close with doubled Ulamogs and Ugin ultimates; Echoes + Unsealing is the escape hatch from stalemates.

B4 Count to a protected combo turn: copier + payoff + Eldrazi spell + activation mana + one piece of protection (or Cavern / Cicada flash). Forced to act early? Battlecruiser mode still clears tables by T14 even when the web never assembles.

Threat assessment: both lists lose most often in their own T5–8 archenemy window. Imprisoned in the Moon buys those turns on the scariest commander — but your own All Is Dust / Ugin −X frees a moon'd commander, so sweep first, moon second.

Methodology. 20,000 games per list, same engine (extends the repo goldfish script): mulligan keeping 2–5 lands; land + greedy rocks/reducers each turn; Ancient Tomb / Eldrazi Temple / Ugin's Labyrinth = 2 mana, Eye of Ugin = −2; commander at 5 + tax; threats attack three 40-life opponents; Ulalek's {C}{C} copy doubles Eldrazi bodies when mana allows. This run adds strict per-turn combo checks of the Spellbook-verified lines, and for B4 models the black tutors (fetch the missing piece of the most complete line) and Urza's Saga. Not modeled: opponent interaction (overstates speed); annihilator, Ulamog mill, cascade, and all draw engines (understates speed and materially understates B4 combo assembly).

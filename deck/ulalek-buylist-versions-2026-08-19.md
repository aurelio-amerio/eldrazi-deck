# Ulalek Buylist by Printing — 2026-08-19 (Cardmarket EUR via Scryfall)

Source list: `deck/ulalek+version.txt` (100 entries, set + collector number as chosen by the owner).
For every entry both the **chosen printing** and the **cheapest printing of the same card** (all sets,
all finishes, non-digital) were priced from the Scryfall API, Cardmarket EUR, snapshot 2026-08-19.

Tiers are set by the **cheapest** price in any version, per the owner's rule:

| Tier | Cheapest € | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | < 1.50 | buy |
| 2 | 1.50 – 2.49 | check before buying |
| 3 | ≥ 2.50 | proxy (appendix) |

**54 of 100 cards are under 2.50 € in some printing.** Buying every tier-1/2 card in its cheapest
printing costs **29.60 €**; buying those same cards in the chosen printings costs **100.27 €**
(five Secret Lair arts account for 64 € of the 71 € gap; the chosen-printing total excludes Erebor
Heirloom, which has no EUR price — see footnote d). ✅ = already in
`archidekt-collection-export-2026-08-17.csv`.

Column notes: `*F*` = foil printing. "= cheapest" means the chosen printing *is* the cheapest one.
Where the cheapest printing carries a different name it is a Secret Lair alternate-name card and the
real card name is shown in italics.

  **MH3 76 nonfoil at 1.26 €** as the realistic cheapest; still tier 1. The chosen MH3 328 is the
  borderless art at 2.61 €.
[^b]: **Tower of Rasmodius = Command Tower**, **Heart of the Mountain = Arcane Signet** (Secret Lair
  alternate names). Their cheapest hits are TMC foils whose EUR (0.13 / 0.28 €) sits far below their
  USD ($28.67 / $22.57) — thin data on a brand-new set. Sane nonfoil alternatives: **Command Tower
  CMR 350 at 0.19 €**, **Arcane Signet OTC 252 at 0.30 €**. Both stay tier 1 either way, so the
  SLD arts (9.90 € / 29.95 €) are proxy-or-splurge, not necessary buys.
[^c]: **Petrified Hamlet** — promo-pack foil at 0.28 € vs $4.74 USD; the nonfoil PSOS 259p is 0.48 €.
  Same printing, so the chosen version is fine at either price.
[^d]: **Erebor Heirloom = Fellwar Stone** (Secret Lair alternate name). SLD 2557 has **no Cardmarket
  EUR price** on Scryfall at this snapshot, so the chosen-version cost is unknown; the plain
  Fellwar Stone is 0.93 €.
